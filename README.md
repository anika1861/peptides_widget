# Three routes to one vial

The interactive widget for MadPharma's November 2026 post on the HHS policy for six unapproved peptides.

For BPC-157, Semax and epitalon, it compares three ways the same peptide can reach a patient: a "research purposes only" website, a pharmacy compounding under the HHS policy, and an FDA-approved drug. For each route it shows whether anyone has to check identity, impurities, endotoxin, evidence of benefit, human safety data, the maker and adverse-event reporting, with a source for each cell.

[index.html](index.html) is self-contained: no external scripts or fonts, light and dark mode, and it works at phone width. Open it in a browser, or serve it with GitHub Pages (Settings, Pages, deploy from the `main` branch, root folder) at <https://anika1861.github.io/peptides_widget/>.

Sources: the HHS enforcement-discretion policy, 91 FR 68526, and its Appendix B; FDA's briefing documents for the Pharmacy Compounding Advisory Committee meeting of 23–24 July 2026; 21 CFR 314.80 and 314.126. As of 4 November 2026.

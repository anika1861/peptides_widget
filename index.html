Three routes to one vial

The same peptide can reach a patient three ways. For BPC-157, Semax and epitalon, this shows which of seven checks each route meets, and the record behind each answer.

    Met means the route requires the check and the record shows it done. Partly met means only part of it is required, or it rests on the supplier's certificate, or the record shows only part. Not met means neither, or, on the FDA route, that no approved product exists. "Not required" means the route sets no such check, not that nobody ever checks. Page numbers are the FDA documents' own.
